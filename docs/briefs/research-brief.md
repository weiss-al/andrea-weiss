# Assessment of Profitability of Incorporating a Pay-What-You-Want Model 

## The topic
I want to evaluate the potential profitability of incorporating a Pay What You Want model for audio tour guide purchases at a museum by local residents. The museum typically sees an overwhelming majority of tourist traffic, with minimal attendance by local residents. 


## What I am assuming
- A given initial visitor count and percentage of local/tourist visitation
- The effectiveness of a marketing campaign to share the new price structure with the local community.
- The data on existing PWYW businesses can be replicated in other communities and businesses.

## Hypothesis
I expect that a Pay What You Want (PWYW) model will be a viable way to increase revenue in audio tours and in the museum overall. The PWYW structure could bring in traffic that otherwise would not attend at all, so any amount they choose to pay would be otherwise lost revenue. Additionally, when they do come to the museum and receive the free audio tour, they are then more likely to spend in the gift shop, which compounds the potential revenue gain.

## Method
Build a model to assess increased revenue potential for the additional visitor traffic. 

## Model
Build an initial set of assumptions for visitor traffic, effectiveness of a marketing campaign, and projected spend at the gift shop for any visitor. After researching other PWYW models, develop assumption for how much local visitors would choose to pay on-average based on social pressure. Let the model evaluate what increased revenue could be expected based on the scale of the marketing campaign.

## How I would know I was wrong
If the cost of the marketing campaign was so high that I could not reach enough visitors to gain any increased revenue, even with a conservative PWYW assumption. 
If local visitor traffic were high enough that shifting existing local visitors to PWYW decreased revenues, even with some new visitors coming in for the promotion.
