# Economic Research — paper specification

<!-- Written by me, before the research starts. Fill each section; delete these comments as you go. -->

## Purpose
What decision the paper supports and what it must be able to answer, in two sentences.

## Inputs — the named contract
| Name | Value | Unit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

Data sources, series, and studies the analysis will draw on. Each gets a name, a value or location, a unit, and a source.

## Structure
Sections of the paper and what each argues. Where each figure sits.

## Analysis logic
Which course concepts do the explaining, by name, and how they connect to the argument. The model or comparison behind each figure.

## Conventions
Citation style, page limits (four pages of body text, not counting title page, figures, bibliography, appendix), typography (double-spaced, 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins), and how sources are verified before they enter the paper.

## Validation rules
Success criteria a finished paper must meet. For example: every AI-supplied number checked against a real source; at least one labeled figure the text actually uses; the recommendation defended against its strongest objection; no repository URL or identifying information on body pages.

## Outputs
The finished paper, its figures, and the closing reflection, by filename.

## Audit findings
Added AFTER the paper is drafted. For each check: what you checked, what you found, what you did about it.
